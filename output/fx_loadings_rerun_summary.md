## Reading

**Nothing moved.** Every waypoint and both dial figures sit inside one seed standard
deviation; the largest |t| is 1.6, on the China-cap step under repression, which is the
smallest and noisiest step in the exhibit. No published conclusion changes.

That is the expected result and it is worth stating why, because it is easy to mistake for a
null change having been applied. The loadings enter the model only through **mean-zero
deviation terms** — `inflation_loading × (π − π̄)` and `growth_loading × (g − ḡ)` — applied to
the unhedged FX share of the portfolio. Over a full lifecycle those deviations average out,
so a loading change shifts the *dispersion* contribution of currency far more than the median
outcome. The individual currency moves also partly offset: eight inflation loadings fall
while JPY's rises.

**The value of this change is provenance, not numbers.** 24 loadings moved from a coefficient
whose origin could not be recovered to one reproduced by a committed script from committed
data, at no cost to any exhibit.

## What did not change

`global_growth_loading` is identical for all 12 currencies. It derives from the residual
growth beta of the same regression and already reproduced exactly — which is what established
that the data, conventions and specification were right, and that only `b_usd` was wrong.

The equity block and the bond sleeves are untouched. The full suite passes, including the
three V-M3 bond-volatility guards.
