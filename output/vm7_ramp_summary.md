# V-M7 — ramped repression transition

**`output/vm7_*.csv` are the current exhibit record.** They supersede `output/fx_*.csv`,
which remain committed as the previous record. Generated by
`python tools/rerun_multiseed.py 1000 vm7` at commit `9689128`, N=1000, five seeds for
the attribution and three for the CGB dial.

`repress()` now runs **12 years pinned with 3-year linear transitions in and out**. The
transitions blend `real_rate` and `inflation` between the path's own value and the pinned
value at weights 1/4, 2/4, 3/4 going in and 3/4, 2/4, 1/4 coming out, rebuilding
`long_rate = real + infl` and `short_rate = long_rate − slope`. The pinned interior is
untouched.

## The deltas are NOT within seed noise — correcting the expectation

The brief expected repression deltas to be unaffected, on the reasoning that every
allocation received the same windfall. **Two of three moved significantly, and so did the
dial.** Splitting the movement into pure level rescaling and genuine change:

| Step | fx Δ | scaled by 0.9159 | vm7 Δ | beyond scaling | Δ as % of level, fx → vm7 | t |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| (ii) +Re-anchor | +81.7 | +74.8 | +72.1 | −2.7 | 8.32% → 8.02% | −2.47 |
| (iii) +China cap | +3.0 | +2.7 | +1.0 | −1.7 | 0.30% → 0.11% | −1.27 |
| (iv) +Bond side | +45.2 | +41.4 | **+52.1** | **+10.6** | **4.61% → 5.79%** | **+4.64** |

**(ii) is almost entirely mechanical.** Deltas are absolute €000, so an 8.4% smaller base
gives an ~8.4% smaller absolute step. Normalised, the re-anchor step barely moves.

**(iv) is genuine, and it runs the other way.** The bond-side step rises by 10.6k *beyond*
the level rescaling, from 4.61% to 5.79% of the Current level — a **26% increase in the
overlay's measured conditional value**, at t = 4.64.

The reason is that the windfall was never neutral across allocations. It accrued to
long-duration EUR core, which is precisely the benchmark the sovereign overlay is measured
against. Flattering the EUR core understated the overlay. Removing the artefact removes the
flattery, and the overlay's value under repression is larger than previously published — not
smaller. The same mechanism raises the CGB dial's repression figure from +1.86k to +4.96k.
